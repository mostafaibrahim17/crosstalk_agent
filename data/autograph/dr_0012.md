# Bit errors from power-supply ripple on a driver rail

A high-speed lane showed a rising bit error rate that suggested trace coupling. The neighbours were well separated. The cause was supply noise: a switching regulator put ripple onto the rail feeding the lane's driver, and that ripple modulated the driver's output. The fix added decoupling at the driver and cleaned up the regulator's feedback layout. No trace-to-trace adjacency was involved, so a spacing or reroute change would not have helped.
