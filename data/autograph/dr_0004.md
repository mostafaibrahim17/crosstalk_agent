# Clock-to-data coupling over a long adjacent run

A data lane developed timing jitter and a rising bit error rate on a board where a clock lane ran beside it for a long span. The two shared the bottom copper layer for about 56 mm at an edge-to-edge gap near 0.4 mm. Because a clock switches continuously with fast edges, it was a strong aggressor even at that spacing, and the coupling read as far-end crosstalk that shifted the victim's edges. The fix rerouted the clock onto an inner layer with a solid reference plane between the two and added ground stitching, removing the long adjacency. The jitter and the errors cleared.
