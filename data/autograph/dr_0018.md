# Crosstalk-like errors traced to a noisy ground bounce

Several lanes near a large digital block showed correlated jitter and errors that looked like mutual coupling. The neighbours were within the spacing guideline. The cause was ground bounce from the digital block's simultaneous switching, shifting the local reference for the nearby lanes. The fix improved the block's power delivery and ground stitching. Rerouting individual lanes apart would not have addressed a shared-reference problem.
