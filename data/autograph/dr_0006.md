# Clock coupling into a serial lane fixed by widening and shielding

A serial data lane showed frequency variation and occasional bit errors traced to a neighbouring clock lane that ran parallel for about 47 mm at roughly 0.36 mm spacing on a shared layer. Because a clock switches continuously, it coupled strongly even at that spacing, and the mechanism read as far-end crosstalk over the long run. Board area allowed a local spacing increase, so the fix pulled the clock and the lane apart to beyond the two-trace-width guideline across the coupled span and added a grounded shield where the pull-apart was not enough. Link margin returned and the errors cleared.
