# Frequency variation traced to an impedance mismatch

A data lane showed frequency variation and an elevated bit error rate that first looked like crosstalk. Investigation found no aggressor running close and parallel. The real cause was an impedance discontinuity: a trace-width change at a connector transition reflected energy back down the line. The fix corrected the trace width for controlled impedance. Spacing to neighbours was already within the routing guideline, so opening it up would not have helped.
