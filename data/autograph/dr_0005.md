# Bit errors cleared by shortening a coupled bus segment

Two parallel high-speed nets on the same outer layer ran together for roughly 60 mm at a gap around 0.33 mm and produced frequency variation and rising BER on the quieter of the two. The mechanism was far-end crosstalk accumulated over the long coupled length. Rather than widen the whole run, the team broke the parallelism: they split the coupled segment with a short jog to another layer so the two nets no longer ran alongside for more than a few millimetres. Reducing the coupled length below the far-end threshold returned the lane to specification.
