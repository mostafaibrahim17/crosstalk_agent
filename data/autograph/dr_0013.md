# Intersymbol interference from an under-equalized channel

A long backplane lane showed a closing eye and rising bit error rate that looked like coupling from a neighbour. Neighbours were spaced within the guideline. The real cause was intersymbol interference: the channel's loss at the signalling rate smeared symbols into one another. The fix enabled transmit de-emphasis and receiver equalization for the channel. Widening trace spacing would not have addressed a loss-driven ISI problem.
